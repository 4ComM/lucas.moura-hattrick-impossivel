Dos 22 em mata-mata, é o único.Lucas Moura is right-footed (Opta profile) and scored all three with his left. Of the 113 hat-tricks in group and knockout stages where Opta records the foot of every goal (from 2010/11 to 02/11/2026), only 3 were entirely with the weaker foot: Messi (left-footed, three right-footed, APOEL, 2014/15), Fermín López (Barcelona, 2025/26) and Lucas (2018/19). Of the 22 in knockout ties, it is the only one.
